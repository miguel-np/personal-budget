# Tax and regulatory research

Use the application's dedicated web-research flow, not the financial MCP tools. The application keeps this workflow separate from personal financial data.

## Required context

Ask the user to specify the applicable jurisdiction, subject, tax year, filing year and reference date. Add autonomous community, municipality, taxpayer type or event date only when relevant and explicitly supplied.

## Procedure

1. Search only after explicit per-query consent.
2. Prefer primary sources such as AEAT, BOE, DGT, TEAC, Seguridad Social and the relevant official regional authority.
3. Distinguish binding legislation, administrative criteria, official guidance and secondary commentary.
4. Check publication date, entry into force, transitional provisions and version applicable to the reference date.
5. Cite the precise official source and identify unresolved discrepancies.
6. Abstain from categorical conclusions when applicability, source support or temporal validity is unresolved.

## Constraints

- Never infer tax residence, taxpayer status, deductions or obligations from budget movements or approximate location.
- Never send movements, accounts, balances, documents or private financial context to a search provider.
- Do not submit declarations, operate certificates or guarantee tax outcomes.
- State that research is informational and does not replace qualified tax/legal advice.
